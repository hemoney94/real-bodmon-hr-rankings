How the ranking works

The page combines Ballpark Pal matchup probability and hitter park/weather factors with recent Baseball Savant contact quality. Recent form uses barrels, hard-hit rate, 105+ and 110+ mph contact, hard airborne contact, max exit velocity, hits and home runs from the eight days before the slate. Average exit velocity is intentionally excluded because weak grounders can hide premium home-run contact. Small samples are pulled toward neutral. Confirmed starters and bench players are both eligible by default; bench players are priced with a lower plate-appearance-odds multiplier since they may not play at all. Tiny and missing Statcast samples are penalized, recent form has reduced influence, lower-order hitters and bench players need evidence of power contact to reach the top tiers, and matchup HR probability is no longer blended with an incompatible projected-HR scale. Pitcher Attack Score uses the starter’s recent zone location, heart-of-zone and meatball rates, barrels and hard contact allowed, and home runs per 100 plate appearances. Overall Zone% receives limited weight; pitches over the heart matter much more.
